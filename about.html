About Agro Trade Hub Africa

Two registered companies. One vision for agricultural growth.

Agro Trade Hub Africa is a collaborative agricultural initiative formed by Verneldos Global Services Ltd and Ntonarche Acumen Network, two registered corporate entities united by a shared commitment to advancing agriculture in Nigeria. Through practical innovation, farmer-focused engagement, and stronger market access, the initiative is designed to support productivity, sustainability, and long-term growth across the agricultural value chain.

Who we are

Agro Trade Hub Africa was established through the coming together of two incorporated organisations: Verneldos Global Services Ltd and Ntonarche Acumen Network. Both companies are registered entities with a common purpose: to promote modern agricultural practices, strengthen rural productivity, and improve access to opportunities for farmers, cooperatives, and agribusiness stakeholders across Nigeria.

Together, the two organisations created Agro Trade Hub Africa as a unified platform for agricultural development. By combining institutional strength, grassroots reach, and a shared commitment to practical impact, they are helping to build a more efficient, resilient, and commercially viable farming ecosystem across the country.

Our story

Agro Trade Hub Africa began as a response to a simple gap: farmers across Nigeria needed a consistent, trustworthy way to reach financial services, buyers and modern farming knowledge — without depending on whichever contact happened to be nearby.

By organising coordinators and secretaries in every state, and linking that network directly to Verneldos and Ntonarche's service lines, the initiative turned a scattered set of relationships into one coordinated platform that any member can call on.

Why Agro Trade Hub Africa

Cooperative societies, financial institutions and commercial partners — including FIS Community Hope MPCS — already work alongside the network to strengthen market access, secure supply chains and improve inclusive participation for smallholders and larger agribusinesses alike.

The Story Behind the Initiative

Our History

Welcome to the official platform of Agro Trade Hub Africa.

How It Began

It started on the ground. On March 18, 2016, a visit to Boki Local Government Area in Cross River State put us face to face with the realities of farming communities. Later, we took part in the Bakor Nation Festival on September 15 and travelled on to Benue State, listening to farmers and seeing first-hand where support was working and where it was falling short.

What began as a single initiative has since grown into a Pan-African agricultural movement.

Research and Discovery

Before building anything, Verneldos Global Services Ltd and Ntonarch Acumen Network, two independent companies, set out to understand the true state of agriculture on the ground. Together, they carried out assessments across Nigeria, conducted feasibility studies, and undertook extensive research into the challenges facing farmers, the gaps in existing support systems, and the opportunities across crop value chains.

The findings led to one clear conclusion: Africa's agriculture needs a funnel, a structured channel that carries training, inputs, finance, and market access directly to practising farmers. Too much support was being lost along the way, and too little of it was reaching the people who actually work the land.

Our Mission

To empower practising farmers across diverse crop value chains through a modern, structured, end-to-end support model.

Today, in strategic partnership with other key sector stakeholders, Verneldos Global Services Ltd and Ntonarch Acumen Network proudly present Agro Trade Hub Africa.

What We Offer

Our services are open to registered members and beyond.

  • Capacity Building & Extension: Practical training in modern, sustainable, high-yield farming practices.
  • Farm Input Support: Direct access to quality inputs that restore soil health and optimise harvest yields.
  • Capital & Credit Access: Facilitated financing to help farmers grow and sustain their operations.
