Membership

Membership built for how you work the land.

Whether you farm, run a cooperative, or supply services into agriculture, there is a membership path built around what you need from the network.

Join Agro Trade Hub Africa

Who can join

Farmers

Smallholders and commercial growers of rice, maize, yam, plantain, cassava, cocoa and other crops.

Cooperatives

Registered cooperative societies and farmer groups coordinating on behalf of their members.

Agribusinesses

Processors, input suppliers, logistics providers and other commercial partners in the value chain.

Benefits of membership

  • Direct line to your state coordinator and secretary.
  • Access to agency banking, loans and financing services.
  • Training, advisory and value-addition support.
  • Discounted access to machinery, seedlings and organic fertiliser.
  • Digital tools for farm management and market information.
  • Support preparing produce for wider and export markets.

Membership categories

Farmer Member

Individual farmers and smallholder groups joining a state network directly.

  • State WhatsApp & coordinator access
  • Loans, agency banking, training
  • Seedling & input advisory

Partner Member

Cooperatives and agribusinesses collaborating on supply, trade and services.

  • Everything in Farmer Member
  • Supply chain & export support
  • Business investment consultancy

Sponsor Member

Organisations backing programmes, training or infrastructure for the network.

  • Programme & community visibility
  • Direct engagement with coordinators
  • Custom partnership terms

Registration fees

  • Farmers and business registration: ₦3,000
  • Group or Association registration: ₦50,000
  • Cooperative registration: ₦25,000
  • Partnership registration with us: ₦200,000
  • Investors: ₦500,000 to ₦10,000,000 and above

How to register

Choose the category that matches you, then fill in the short form. Fill in your application, then click Proceed to Pay. You will be redirected to secure Paystack checkout.

What happens after you submit? Agro Trade Hub Africa will confirm your payment and get back to you within 24 hours. We will also send a confirmation email to the address you provide.

Before starting any membership registration, review the payment and refund terms.

Select a membership category above to open its application form.
